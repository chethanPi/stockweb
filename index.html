Value Investing Guardrails & SEBI Compliance

1. Graham Number:

Calculated as √(22.5 × EPS × BVPS). Benjamin Graham considered stocks trading below this multiple as protective against severe capital impairment.

2. Margin of Safety (MoS %):

The percentage discount between current stock price and conservative intrinsic valuation. A 20% to 30% margin absorbs macro shocks and earnings surprises.

3. Economic Moats:

Pricing power, high switching costs, or scale economies that allow a business to sustain ROCE > 18% over a complete economic cycle without dilution.
